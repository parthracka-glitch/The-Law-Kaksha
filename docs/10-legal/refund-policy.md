## 1. Digital Content Delivery Model

All educational offerings on The Law Kaksha—including Chapter Notes, Statutory Codices, 1.5-Day LDR Charts, and Objective Question Banks—are delivered digitally via immediate in-browser DRM fulfillment upon verified payment.

---

## 2. Cancellation & Refund Eligibility

1. **Digital Nature of Goods:** Because complete digital study notes and proprietary examination strategies are made available and readable immediately upon payment confirmation, purchases are generally **non-refundable** once access is granted.
2. **Duplicate Payment Protection:** In the event that a technical glitch, gateway timeout, or dual-click results in duplicate debits for the identical course order, the excess payment will be refunded in full.
   - **Reporting Window:** Within 48 hours of transaction.
   - **Resolution Timeline:** Refund initiated via Razorpay back to the source bank account / UPI within 5–7 business days.
3. **Severe Technical Inaccessibility:** If a candidate is unable to access purchased materials due to a persistent server fault on our side that our engineering team cannot resolve within 72 hours of written notice, a prorated or full refund will be provided.
