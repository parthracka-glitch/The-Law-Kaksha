---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy defines the rules governing candidate conduct, digital rights management (DRM), and acceptable behavior on **The Law Kaksha** learning portal. It applies to all visitors, enrolled candidates, and administrative users.

---

## 2. Permitted Academic Use

Enrolled candidates are granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable personal academic license to:
- Read, navigate, and study purchased digital codices in the in-browser 3D reader on an authorized single device.
- Take interactive practice mock tests and review feedback rubrics.
- Submit academic questions to faculty through authorized channels.

---

## 3. Strictly Prohibited Activities

The following actions constitute material violations of this policy and will result in immediate subscription termination, permanent device bans, and potential statutory legal action under the *Information Technology Act, 2000* and the *Indian Copyright Act, 1957*:

1. **Copyright Infringement & Redistribution:**
   - Capturing, screen-recording, taking automated screenshots, or printing study codices for redistribution.
   - Sharing, uploading, or circulating course materials on Telegram groups, WhatsApp communities, Google Drives, or public websites.
2. **Account & Credential Sharing:**
   - Disclosing login passwords or OTPs to third parties.
   - Bypassing or attempting to disable single-device DRM session locks.
3. **Automated Scraping & Security Probing:**
   - Utilizing bots, scrapers, web spiders, or automated scripts to harvest study notes, question banks, or student rosters.
   - Attempting SQL injection, NoSQL injection, parameter tampering, or brute-force attacks against portal APIs.
4. **Harassment & Misconduct:**
   - Submitting abusive, defamatory, or hostile messages to academic faculty or support desks.

---

## 4. Enforcement & Reporting

Violations may be reported directly to our security and compliance desk:
- **Email:** `security@thelawkaksha.com` / `grievance@thelawkaksha.com`
- **Action:** Investigated within 48 business hours. Proven infringements result in account revocation without refund.
