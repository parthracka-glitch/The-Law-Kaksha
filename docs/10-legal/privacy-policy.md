## 2. Personal Data We Collect

1. **Account & Identity Data:** Full name, email address, mobile phone number, chosen target exam (CA Foundation or CSEET).
2. **Transactional & Purchase Information:** Purchased study codices, subscription active status, order receipts, billing summaries. (We do not store payment card numbers or banking passwords; these are processed securely by Razorpay).

We process your data strictly under the following lawful grounds:

## 4. Single-Device DRM & Anti-Piracy Protection

To protect our proprietary legal notes and academic summaries from unauthorized reproduction:
- Each candidate account is authorized to view digital notes on **one primary active device** at any time.
- When you log in from a new or secondary device, the platform prompts you to transfer access, revoking active reading permissions on the prior device.
- Study materials in the DRM reader are dynamically watermarked with your unique Student Roll Number (`student_id`) to deter screenshots and unauthorized file dumping.

## 5. Data Subject Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:
1. **Right to Access & Summary:** Request a summary of personal data held about you.
2. **Right to Correction:** Update incorrect personal or contact details via `support@thelawkaksha.com`.
3. **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your account and personal profile upon completion of your exams, subject to statutory tax retention requirements.
