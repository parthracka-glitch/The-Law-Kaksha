## 1. Candidate Onboarding Guide

### Step 1: Browse & Choose Your Syllabus
- Visit [thelawkaksha.com](https://thelawkaksha.com).
- Explore our specialized offerings:
  - **CA Foundation (Paper 2: Business Laws):** Indian Regulatory Framework, Contract Act 1872, Sale of Goods Act 1930, Partnership Act 1932, LLP Act 2008, Companies Act 2013.
  - **CSEET:** Legal Aptitude, Business Communication, and Constitutional frameworks.
- Review sample codices and Section 16(1) comparative study flowcharts.

### Step 2: Instant Enrollment & Payment
- Click **Enroll Now** on your desired course.
- Fill in your Full Name, Email, and Phone Number (used for single-device verification).
- Complete checkout securely via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or Net Banking through Razorpay.
- Upon payment confirmation, your account is provisioned immediately.

### Step 3: Accessing Your Study Dashboard
- Navigate to `/student` or click **Student Dashboard** in the top navigation.
- If not logged in, sign in using your registered email and password.
- Your purchased course codices appear under **My Enrolled Courses** with an active **Open Codex** button.

### Step 4: Reading in the Protected 3D Reader
- Click **Open Codex** on any unlocked volume.
- Use arrow keys, on-screen navigation buttons, or touch swipe to flip through pages.
- Note: Study codices are watermarked with your student roll ID and name to safeguard proprietary academic research.

## 2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Account & Device Binding
**Q1: Can I study on both my phone and my laptop?**  
*A:* To safeguard student accounts and proprietary materials, The Law Kaksha enforces a single active device lock. However, you can freely switch between devices! When logging in on a second device, check "Transfer session to this device" on the login screen. Your previous device will be logged out automatically.

**Q2: What should I do if I forgot my password?**  
*A:* Click **Forgot Password?** on the login page. Enter your registered email address, and a secure password reset link will be dispatched to your inbox.

### Study Notes & Codex Reader
**Q3: Can I download the study notes as PDF files to print?**  
*A:* No. The Law Kaksha study codices are designed for interactive, structured digital learning inside our proprietary 3D in-browser reader with full search and high-resolution rendering. Direct raw PDF downloads are disabled to protect against unauthorized commercial distribution.

**Q4: Do the study notes reflect the latest ICAI syllabus changes?**  
*A:* Yes! All codices are curated and updated regularly to match the latest study guidelines and statutory pronouncements issued by the ICAI Board of Studies.

### Payments & Subscriptions
**Q5: What payment methods are accepted?**  
*A:* All major Indian payment methods are supported through Razorpay: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Debit/Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), and Net Banking across 50+ Indian banks.

**Q6: I made a payment, but my notes are still locked. What should I do?**  
*A:* Payment verification usually completes within seconds. If a network disruption occurs, refresh your dashboard after 30 seconds. If access is still pending, email your payment reference ID to `support@thelawkaksha.com` or WhatsApp our student helpline for immediate manual activation.
